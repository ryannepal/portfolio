DOCUMENTS GO IN THIS FOLDER
===========================

Your resume lives here as:

  Ryan-Nepal-Resume.pdf

It is linked from two places in content.json:
  "profile" > "buttons"  -> the "Download resume" button in the hero
  "contact" > "resume"   -> the Resume row in the Contact section

TO UPDATE YOUR RESUME
  Easiest way: rename your new PDF to exactly Ryan-Nepal-Resume.pdf and
  replace the file here. Nothing in content.json needs to change.

  If you'd rather keep the new file name, upload it here and change both
  paths in content.json to files/Your-New-Name.pdf

You can also drop reports, drawings, or STEP files in here and link them from
a project's "links" list, e.g.
  { "label": "Report (PDF)", "url": "files/fuel-pump-adapter-report.pdf" }
